LATEST TECHNOLOGY DEVELOPMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

1. Esri's EO-DINO model enables organizations to extract information from remote imagery with less labeled data, enhancing environmental monitoring capabilities. [Source](https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/products/arcgis/geoai/whats-new-in-geospatial-ai-november-2026)
2. NASA's Prithvi model, the first AI geospatial foundation model in orbit, is designed for various Earth observation tasks and has been tested for flood and cloud detection. [Source](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/ai-foundation-model-in-orbit)
3. Google's Earth AI has evolved to include a Geospatial Reasoning agent capable of complex spatial analysis and planetary predictions, expanding access for developers. [Source](https://research.google/blog/putting-earth-ai-to-work-geospatial-agents-for-our-planets-biggest-challenges)
4. PwC and Esri have expanded their alliance to integrate AI and geospatial insights into enterprise operations, particularly for energy and infrastructure sectors. [Source](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/pwc-esri-intelligent-enterprise.html)
5. AI is transforming land surveying by automating data processing, significantly reducing manual work and improving efficiency in project delivery. [Source](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/29/3391391/0/en/ai-transforming-land-surveying-as-billions-in-geospatial-and-construction-markets-fuel-new-growth-opportunities.html)
